2. How to send a takedown notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorised agent and you believe a file on this site infringes your rights, email a complete notice to dmca@example.com (replace with your address) with the subject line “DMCA Takedown”.

Your notice should include:

  • Your full legal name, organisation, postal address, telephone number, and email.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work (ISBN, title, publisher, and a URL of an authorised edition if available).
  • The exact URL(s) on this site where the allegedly infringing file or listing appears.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

3. What we do next

On receipt of a complete notice we will:

  • Acknowledge the email within two business days where possible.
  • Remove or disable access to the specified material promptly.
  • Note the takedown internally so the file is not restored by mistake.
  • Where we have a publisher of the file, notify them of the complaint.

4. Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe this was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same address including your contact details, identification of the removed material and its former URL, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the removal was a mistake, consent to the jurisdiction of the relevant courts, and your signature. We may restore the material after 10–14 business days unless the original complainant seeks a court order.
