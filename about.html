The project

About Us

LernBücher is a calm, multilingual shelf for people who are learning German — in school, at work, or at the kitchen table. We organise titles the way learners actually search: by language of explanation and by CEFR level.

Why three libraries?

German courses are not one-size-fits-all. A teenager in Berlin, a family from the Balkans, and a professional who thinks in Russian need different notes around the same grammar. That is why the navigation uses native names:

  • Deutsche Bücher — materials written in German for immersion and exam practice.
  • Немецкие книги — German with Russian explanations, tables, and exam strategy.
  • Libra Gjermanisht — German with Albanian support for learners and parents.

How the catalog works

Every title lives in one JavaScript file so editors can add a cover, a 2–3 line description, a level badge, and a PDF path without touching the layout. Search is instant and runs in the browser — no account, no tracking pixel in this template.

Our commitments

  • Keep the interface readable on a phone first.
  • Label levels honestly (A1 through C2).
  • Host only files we have the right to share, and honour takedown requests on the DMCA page.
