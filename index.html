How to download

Three steps. No account required.

  1. 1 Open a category — Deutsche Bücher, Немецкие книги, or Libra Gjermanisht — or search by title and CEFR level.
  2. 2 Read the short description and confirm the level badge matches your class or exam target.
  3. 3 Tap Download PDF. Your browser saves the file. On phones, check Downloads or Files.
